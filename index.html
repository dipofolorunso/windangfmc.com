What We Offer

Comprehensive General Practice Services

Family & Preventive Care

Health checks, immunisations, wellness screenings and preventive care for every member of your family, from newborns through to seniors.

Chronic Disease Management

Ongoing support and care plans for diabetes, heart disease, asthma, arthritis and other chronic conditions, including Care Plan referrals.

Acute & General Illness

Same-day or priority appointments for urgent health concerns, infections, injuries and acute medical conditions.

Mental Health & ADHD

Compassionate mental health assessments, Mental Health Care Plans, ADHD assessments, and referrals to psychologists and allied health professionals.

Women's Health

Cervical screening, contraception, antenatal shared care, menopause management and a full range of women's health services.

Child & Infant Health

Childhood development checks, vaccinations, growth monitoring and care for common childhood illnesses and concerns.

Aged Care & Senior Health

Comprehensive health assessments for older Australians, aged care referrals, falls prevention and dementia support.

Immunisations & Travel Health

Full adult and childhood vaccination programs including flu shots, COVID-19 boosters and the National Immunisation Program. Travel health consultations and vaccinations including yellow fever vaccines are also available.

Skin Checks & Minor Procedures

Comprehensive skin examinations for early detection of skin cancers and suspicious lesions. We also perform minor skin procedures including skin tag removal, cyst treatment and biopsies, with referrals for further treatment as needed.

Referrals & Specialist Care

Referrals to specialists, allied health professionals, pathology, radiology and coordination of your overall care journey.

Insurance & Work Medicals

Comprehensive medicals for workers compensation, CTP claims, pre-employment assessments, commercial driver licences and other insurance purposes. Private fees apply.

In-Clinic Diagnostic Tests

A range of diagnostic tests performed on-site including ECG, Holter monitoring, ambulatory blood pressure monitoring, ankle brachial pressure index (ABPI) and spirometry.

Nursing Services

Dedicated nursing care including wound management, ear syringing, immunisation administration, health assessments, chronic disease care plans and point-of-care testing.

Government Initiative

What is MyMedicare?

MyMedicare is a free, voluntary patient registration scheme introduced by the Australian Government to strengthen the relationship between patients and their regular GP practice.

  • Greater continuity of care with your registered GP and practice, improving health outcomes
  • Access to longer Medicare-funded telehealth consultations (phone or video) with your GP
  • Better support for chronic and complex conditions through linked Chronic Disease Management items
  • Registration is completely voluntary and free — you can remain our patient whether or not you register

Register with Windang FMC

Windang Family Medical Centre is registered with MyMedicare. Registering is simple and takes just a few minutes. Ask our friendly reception staff at your next visit.

Your Visit

Patient Information

Everything you need to know before, during and after your visit to Windang Family Medical Centre.

Fees

All services are bulk billed for patients with a current Medicare card — no out-of-pocket costs.

Private fees apply for patients without a Medicare card:

Standard consult (<20 min)$60
Long consult (20–40 min)$100
Prolonged consult (>40 min)$140

Private fees also apply to insurance medicals and commercial drivers' licence medicals. Payment accepted by cash, EFTPOS or direct deposit on the day.

Appointments

Booking: Call us on 02 4201 0288 or book online via HotDoc. Request a long appointment if you need extra time.
Telehealth: Available for regular patients in some circumstances. Please discuss with reception.
Walk-ins: Accepted, however scheduled patients are prioritised — wait times may be longer.
Home visits: Offered in certain circumstances at the GP's discretion. Please discuss with reception.

Communication

Contacting us: Our reception team can assist with most queries. The preferred mode of contact is by phone. A doctor or nurse may follow up in some cases.
Results: We may contact you by SMS, phone or mail. Please keep your contact details up to date and opt in for SMS communication.
Medical certificates: An appointment is required. Certificates cannot be backdated.
Referrals: New and repeat specialist referrals must be requested during your consultation.

Emergencies

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, please call 000 immediately.
The nearest hospital is Shellharbour Public Hospital, 15–17 Madigan Blvd, Mount Warrigal NSW 2528.
For after-hours medical treatment, contact Radio Doctor Illawarra on 02 4228 5522.

Onsite Pathology

Laverty Pathology collection services are available onsite at Windang Family Medical Centre.
No need to travel elsewhere for your blood tests or other pathology — our onsite Laverty collection centre makes it convenient to complete your tests in one visit.
Pathology requests must be obtained from your GP prior to collection. Please book an appointment if you do not have a current request.
For Laverty Pathology hours and further information please ask at reception.

Privacy

Our practice takes its confidentiality and privacy obligations seriously. We handle your personal health information in accordance with Australian privacy law.
Please speak to our reception staff to view our full privacy policy.
We may use electronic communication (email or SMS) to send you important health information.
